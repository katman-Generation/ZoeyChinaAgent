How It Works

1

Tell Us What You Need. Share your exact product specifications, reference images, target pricing, and estimated order quantities (MOQ) with our team.

2

Supplier Matching & Quotations We search our localized manufacturing networks to vet factories, negotiate direct-from-manufacturer pricing, and present you with the best competitive quotes.

3

Sample Development & Approval Before you wire any bulk deposits, we secure product samples from the factory. We check them in-office or ship them to you to guarantee the materials and specifications are flawless.

4

Direct Order Placement & Price NegotiationOnce you give the green light, we finalize the contracts, manage localized payment processing securely, and oversee the mass production timeline directly on the factory floor.

5

Strict Pre-Shipment Inspection Our "boots on the ground " team conducts hands-on quality control (QC) checks at the factory before loading. We send you comprehensive photo and video evidence so you know your order is perfect.

6

Safe Global Shipping & LogisticsWe consolidate your goods at our warehouse, handle all Chinese customs declarations and export paperwork, and arrange hassle-free shipping directly to your destination port.
